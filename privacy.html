IISM Autofil

What this extension stores

Profile fields are stored with Chrome's local extension storage. Applicant-selected documents are stored in this browser's extension database. The extension does not operate a profile server or an analytics service.

If you tick “Use my other computers”, your typed details are also kept in your Google account by Chrome's own sync feature, so Google stores them. Chrome, not this extension, handles that copy, and ticking the box off deletes it. Documents are never synced.

Data is used only to fill the IISM admission form after you click the fill button. The extension does not read passwords, OTPs, cookies, or unrelated browsing pages.

When information leaves this browser

When you choose to submit the application on the IISM website, the form data and attached documents are sent to that website under its own terms and privacy practices. The extension does not submit the form or send application data to its developer.

Control and deletion

Use “Clear saved data” in the side panel to remove the saved profile and documents. Removing the extension also removes its browser-local data. When “Use my other computers” is on, “Clear saved data” also deletes the copy kept by Chrome sync.
